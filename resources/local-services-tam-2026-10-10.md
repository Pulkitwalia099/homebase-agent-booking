# GoLocal local-services market sizing — October 10, 2026

## Recommended line

**“More than $1 trillion is spent annually in the U.S. across home improvement and repair, auto maintenance and repair, personal care and clothing services, and child day care.”** This is an underlying spending pool, not GoLocal's addressable fee revenue. If a rounded estimate is useful in discussion, **roughly $1.2 trillion** is the sum of the categories below, with mixed observation years and a projected home figure. Do not call it an independently published “$1.2T local-services market.”

| Distinct U.S. category | Amount | Basis and scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Home improvement and repair | ~$600B | Harvard JCHS reports property owners spent over $600B in 2023; its 2025 report projected $608B in 2025 for owner and rental stock. Includes materials and DIY activity. Not all of this is bookable service labor. |
| Motor vehicle maintenance and repair | $283.8B | BEA 2025 personal consumption expenditure, NIPA Table 2.4.5, line 70. |
| Personal care and clothing services | $239.6B | BEA 2025 personal consumption expenditure, NIPA Table 2.4.5, line 105. This broad category is not limited to salon appointments. |
| Child day care | $65.5B | Census Annual Integrated Economic Survey 2024 revenue, NAICS 6244, all establishments. Different measure and year from BEA PCE. |
| **Indicative sum** | **~$1.19T** | Rounded home baseline plus the three additional categories. This is a cross-source approximation, not an official TAM series. |

These four groups are conceptually distinct, so the sum avoids counting restaurant, travel, medical, rent, utility or insurance spending just to inflate the headline. The broader local economy can exceed this amount, but a $2T GoLocal-relevant market has not been established. BEA alone reports $1.258T in 2025 purchased meals and beverages; adding restaurant spending would lift a broad “local commerce” tally above $2T while changing the category and competitive story.

The Harvard figure is a broad remodeling and repair spend estimate. Its latest 2026 LIRA projection of $518B covers **owner-occupied** improvements and maintenance, so it is not directly comparable with the earlier owner-plus-rental ~$600B estimate. Recheck the same-scope annual figure before putting a year-specific ~$600B claim on a slide.

## What GoLocal can address

The **transactionable market** is the part of that spend tied to tasks an agent can discover, quote and book through participating providers with permitted access. GoLocal's **revenue TAM** is smaller again: eligible booked jobs × attainable fee per job, or another agreed payment model. Neither fraction has been measured. Child care, for example, has licensing, trust and enrollment workflows that may make it a later category, not an initial booking wedge. Home improvement materials and DIY spend are also outside a pure service-booking fee base.

For an investor slide, use **“$1T+ in U.S. annual spending across selected local-service categories”** with a footnote naming the categories and years. If the slide is specifically about bookable services, lead with the category and use the broader figure only as context. Replace the unsupported blanket **“local services is a $2T market”** when the user next revises the user-edited thesis or deck.
