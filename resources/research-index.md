## Corrections to carry forward

- The personal-agent launches and funding rounds indicate activity, not transaction volume. The “20+ agents in one month,” aggregate funding, 1% online penetration and 0.001% provider-share claims are unverified; no Wajo funding figure was established.
- Harvard's $600 billion-plus estimate concerns U.S. home improvement and repair spending, including materials; it is not the addressable market for cleaning or an agent-booking fee pool.
- The short thesis's $2T local-services line is founder-supplied wording, not a documented category sum. The [October 10 market-size check](local-services-tam-2026-10-10.md) supports $1T+ across selected categories, with an indicative ~$1.2T mixed-year sum. Neither amount is GoLocal's monetizable TAM.
- Angi and Handy already offer instant booking in some home categories. The proposed opportunity is a neutral, merchant-authorized route across fragmented suppliers and software, whose breadth and value remain unproven.
- Merchant OAuth and a software connection do not guarantee live price, accepted availability or a confirmed job. A data flywheel requires merchant rights, reliable feedback and repeatable onboarding.
- Agreement among agents is not independent demand validation. Histories are small, incomplete and mostly concern one founder.
- Public-path probes occurred; end-to-end commercial execution was not validated. Interface friction does not prove that a missing API caused failure.
- Weekly cleaning does not imply a weekly monetizable agent transaction. New-provider acquisition and existing-customer administration have different economics.
- A hundred suppliers plus one platform integration does not establish venture scale. Earlier sample sizes, pilot thresholds and economic inputs were proposed assumptions.
- Boston cleaning is provisional. Launch27 portals/forms are publicly visible at MaidThis Boston and Maid Sailors; Unmess links to BookingKoala. This is not market-share evidence or proof of production API access.
- Jobber and Housecall Pro are candidates, not chosen infrastructure. Merchant authority, vendor permissions, actual operations and booking acceptance all need verification.
- No outreach, merchant enrollment, production integration or live booking has been recorded in this assessment. Possible introductions to Wajo and the company referred to as Instant/Instinct are not commitments.

## Personal agent protocol developments assessed October 6 2026

The founder's stated focus is durable, venture-scale middleware for personal agents, with local services one possible application. The new [research assessment](../exa-results/personal-agent-middleware-2026-10-06/research.md) proposes a broader comparison without replacing the user-edited discussion thesis.

Partner participation is not evidence of deployment or request volume. Decagon is joining its working group; do not presume a settled standards split.

Auth0, Composio, Temporal, Stripe, Shopify and Cloudflare provide adjacent infrastructure; the memo links their primary documentation and distinguishes their scopes.
- Instinct's participation updates the earlier absence of public interoperability detail. Its marketplace/recommendation rules and any GoLocal routing relationship remain unestablished.
- The proposed opportunity is domain-specific transaction execution, exception handling and outcome reconciliation. Travel changes, complex returns/warranties and property coordination are discovery candidates. Freight/logistics is a separate business-agent adjacency, not verified personal-agent demand.
