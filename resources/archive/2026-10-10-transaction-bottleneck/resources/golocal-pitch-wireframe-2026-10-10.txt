GoLocal pitch structure — October 10, 2026

BUSINESS
GoLocal is the shared data and booking layer for home services. It recruits participating suppliers, connects their authorized business software, and gives personal agents one endpoint to compare providers, request quotes, check schedules, book, pay and manage jobs. These are proposed capabilities. Proposed revenue: a supplier-paid fee on a fulfilled job from a new customer, with a share for the partner bringing demand.

1. THE MARKET, PLATFORM UNLOCKS AND REMAINING BOTTLENECK
Headline: ≈$1.2T U.S. local-services opportunity.
Personal agents can take on the task. The transaction is still fragmented.
Editable matrix with technology/platform columns and green unlocks:
- Pre-2000: Telephones / Yellow Pages / find and call.
- 2000–2010: Internet / Google and Craigslist / search and compare.
- 2010–2020: Mobile + GPS / Google Maps / nearby results and reviews.
- 2020–2025: LLMs / AI chatbots / describe needs and get answers.
- 2026+: AI + tools / personal AI agents / delegate tasks.
Three red workflow rows span the eras: scope/quote, live availability, and book/pay. In the agent column these still require calls, websites and separate checkout. Red means external work, not that an agent cannot do it. Green marks the platform unlock, not universal transaction completion.
Closing point: delegation still leaves calls, waiting and separate checkouts. Agents need structured business data and a reliable way to transact.
Market estimate comes from the October 10 local-services TAM chat and sourced note. Approximately $1.2T in mixed-year U.S. spending across home improvement/repair, auto repair, personal/clothing services and child day care. Includes materials and DIY; not GoLocal revenue TAM. Use approximation rather than claim a precise $1.2T+ lower bound.
No GoLocal name or Stripe analogy on slide 1. Eras are illustrative, not launch dates. Some platforms and businesses already transact.

3. WHAT GOLOCAL BUILDS
Agent labels: Instinct, Muse and Other agents. Instinct replaces Dots per the founder’s annotation. These are illustrative agent platforms, not signed demand partnerships.
Native editable diagram: multiple personal agents ↔ GoLocal ↔ multiple FSM/CRM/booking systems ↔ participating businesses.
GoLocal central data: services and coverage, prices/quote requirements, live slots, permitted transaction history. Actions: quote, book, pay, manage.
System labels are placeholders for integration categories, not signed vendors. Merchant authorization is required; business systems remain authoritative.
Calls query providers sequentially; GoLocal can compare connected supply through one query.
Illustrative target ~15 min → under 5 sec for data lookup only, not measured and not total booking/scoping latency.

4. BUSINESS MODEL AND DISTRIBUTION
Proposed supplier-paid success fee on a fulfilled job from a new customer, with an agreed share for the demand partner. Terms remain open.
Route to explore: direct partnership with a personal-agent platform to route relevant requests.
Route to explore: user-added connector, marketed to a niche with urgent recurring pain. Installation still requires customer acquisition. A single prompt may initiate setup where supported; platform-specific permissions and integration steps can follow.
Native sequence: approved connector → useful completed tasks → broader discovery. Recommendation is conditional on platform rules, not guaranteed from transaction history or early arrival.
Cactus supplies merchant access; it is not the secured personal-agent demand partner.
