GoLocal pitch structure — October 10, 2026

BUSINESS
GoLocal is the shared data and booking layer for home services. It recruits participating suppliers, connects their authorized business software, and gives personal agents one endpoint to compare providers, request quotes, check schedules, book, pay and manage jobs. These are proposed capabilities. Proposed revenue: a supplier-paid fee on a fulfilled job from a new customer, with a share for the partner bringing demand.

1. THE LOCAL-SERVICE JOURNEY — REFINED
GoLocal: Stripe for home-service bookings.
Headline: Each tech era changed the front door to local services.
Approximate eras: Pre-1990s Yellow Pages; 1990s Google/Craigslist; 2000s–2010s Google Maps; 2020s AI agents; Next (planned) AI agents + GoLocal.
Six rows: find business; compare options; check location; scope/quote; check slots/book; pay/manage. Availability and booking merged. Comparison remains separate from scoping to avoid suggesting web filtering completed quote workflows.
Green check = the platform handles the step. Red dash = calls/external work still required in the typical fragmented flow. Agent transaction cells explicitly say Agent calls or Sites/calls. GoLocal column is a planned integrated workflow for authorized participating supply.
Soft cream background, muted red/green cell fills, prominent GoLocal column and light gutters, native editable table. Approximate eras are not precise invention or adoption years, or a regular ten-year cycle. Some platforms already transact; complex quotes still need business input.
Sources: Craigslist official history (1995); Google Maps official retrospective (2005 desktop, mobile expansion 2007–2012). Links in slide notes and research index.

3. WHAT GOLOCAL BUILDS
Native editable diagram: multiple personal agents ↔ GoLocal ↔ multiple FSM/CRM/booking systems ↔ participating businesses.
GoLocal central data: services and coverage, prices/quote requirements, live slots, permitted transaction history. Actions: quote, book, pay, manage.
System labels are placeholders for integration categories, not signed vendors. Merchant authorization is required; business systems remain authoritative.
Calls query providers sequentially; GoLocal can compare connected supply through one query.
Illustrative target ~15 min → under 5 sec for data lookup only, not measured and not total booking/scoping latency.

Proposed supplier-paid success fee on a fulfilled job from a new customer, with an agreed share for the demand partner. Terms remain open.
