GoLocal pitch structure — October 10, 2026

BUSINESS
GoLocal is the shared data and booking layer for home services. It recruits participating suppliers, connects their authorized business software, and gives personal agents one endpoint to compare providers, request quotes, check schedules, book, pay and manage jobs. These are proposed capabilities. Proposed revenue: a supplier-paid fee on a fulfilled job from a new customer, with a share for the partner bringing demand.

1. THE LOCAL-SERVICE JOURNEY
GoLocal: Stripe for home-service bookings.
Native editable matrix: seven customer tasks across Yellow Pages, Google/web listings, Maps, personal agents, and proposed GoLocal tools.
Tasks: find business; compare options; check coverage; scope/quote; check slots; book; pay/manage.
Main point: each platform handled more of discovery, comparison and location. Transactions still depend on specific business systems. Agents can call or browse; GoLocal adds structured supplier data and consistent actions.
Typical workflow, not a claim that every existing platform lacks bookings/payments. Complex scopes may require business input. No chronological product-invention claim.

3. WHAT GOLOCAL BUILDS
Native editable diagram: multiple personal agents ↔ GoLocal ↔ multiple FSM/CRM/booking systems ↔ participating businesses.
GoLocal central data: services and coverage, prices/quote requirements, live slots, permitted transaction history. Actions: quote, book, pay, manage.
System labels are placeholders for integration categories, not signed vendors. Merchant authorization is required; business systems remain authoritative.
Calls query providers sequentially; GoLocal can compare connected supply through one query.
Illustrative target ~15 min → under 5 sec for data lookup only, not measured and not total booking/scoping latency.
